BPMNOS::Execution::SeededGreedyController Class Reference

SeededController realising the seeded decision order greedily. More...

Detailed Description

Entries and exits are taken as soon as they are feasible, choices are made by bisection (falling back to enumeration), and a message is delivered to the best matching receive.
