BPMNOS::Execution::Deferrals Class Reference

The deferred decision points of a decision maker, each held back until a reconsideration. More...

Detailed Description

A decision point is the performer of sequential activities for their entries, a catch event for the delivery of a message, or a decision task for its choice, and is represented by its token: the performer token, the token at the catch event or the token at the decision task. A token at a sequential activity is resolved to its performer token, so that deferring it and asking whether it is deferred concern its performer. Deferring a decision point defers each of its pending requests, for a performer the entry requests of every token awaiting entry at it, together with the deferral of the model stated for the decision point.

A deferral ends at its first reconsideration, which reaches the deferrals as a notification: a new request at the decision point, a new message which may be delivered to a deferred catch event, a data update naming an observed attribute of the deferral, a data update touching an attribute the condition reads after which the condition no longer holds, and a clock tick at which the deferral is no longer admissible. A deferral whose requests have all expired withholds nothing and is removed when next met. An installed system state ends every deferral, since none of the deferred tokens exists in it.

Whether a decision point is deferred is a choice of a decision maker, which holds the deferrals and subscribes them to the notifications of the engine, and offers a deferral only where its condition holds and a deferral is admissible.
