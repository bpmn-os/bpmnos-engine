17/**
18 * @brief The status and the owned data of a scope instance, built when the instance comes into being.
19 *
20 * The constructor lays out a slot for every own attribute of the scope, sets the timestamp to the time at
21 * which the instance comes into being and gives the instance its identity, if it has one of its own. It then
22 * walks over the own attributes of the scope in declaration order, the scalar status attributes, the scalar
23 * data attributes and the objects, and gives each its initial value: the value of its initial expression if
24 * the model initialises it, otherwise the value given, if any. An expression is evaluated over the status and
25 * the data built so far, so that it reads everything inherited, the timestamp, the instance and everything
26 * declared before it, and nothing declared after it.
27 *
28 * A data provider builds the values of a scope with a ready event when the event becomes due and applies its
29 * ready values through @ref assign; the engine builds the values of the scopes it creates itself. The status
30 * and the owned data are then moved into the ready event or into the scope created.
31 */
