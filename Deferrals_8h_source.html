21/**
22 * @brief The deferred decision points of a decision maker, each held back until a reconsideration.
23 *
24 * A decision point is the performer of sequential activities for their entries, a catch event for the delivery of a
25 * message, or a decision task for its choice, and is represented by its token: the performer token, the token at the
26 * catch event or the token at the decision task. A token at a sequential activity is resolved to its performer token,
27 * so that deferring it and asking whether it is deferred concern its performer. Deferring a decision point defers each
28 * of its pending requests, for a performer the entry requests of every token awaiting entry at it, together with the
29 * deferral of the model stated for the decision point.
30 *
31 * A deferral ends at its first reconsideration, which reaches the deferrals as a notification: a new request at the
32 * decision point, a new message which may be delivered to a deferred catch event, a data update naming an observed
33 * attribute of the deferral, a data update touching an attribute the condition reads after which the condition no
34 * longer holds, and a clock tick at which the deferral is no longer admissible. A deferral whose requests have all
35 * expired withholds nothing and is removed when next met. An installed system state ends every deferral, since none of
36 * the deferred tokens exists in it.
37 *
38 * Whether a decision point is deferred is a choice of a decision maker, which holds the deferrals and subscribes them to
39 * the notifications of the engine, and offers a deferral only where its condition holds and a deferral is admissible.
40 */
