20/**
21 * @brief A controller playing back the decisions of a log.
22 *
23 * The log is the JSON output of the engine, an array of records each tagged by its kind, of which the
24 * controller reads the clock ticks and the decisions and ignores every other record. A decision is a record
25 * tagged `event` whose object states its kind by the key `decision`: the entry of a child of a sequential ad-hoc
26 * subprocess, the choices made at a decision task, or the delivery of a message that is not directly addressed.
27 * Its reward is not read. A decision belongs to the time of the last clock tick before it, and a decision before
28 * the first clock tick to the time the run starts. The engine carries out everything that is not a decision
29 * itself, so that a log recorded by the engine and a log stating its decisions alone are played back alike.
30 *
31 * A decision is due once the run has reached its time, and a decision without a time, before the first clock
32 * tick, from the start of the run. At every fetch, the controller dispatches the first due decision, in the order of
33 * the log, whose request is pending, matched by process, instance and node, and for a message delivery by a
34 * message among the candidates of the request agreeing with the origin, the header and the content the log
35 * states. An entry waits while an entry listed before it for the same performer, the performer node of the
36 * sequential ad-hoc subprocess within the instance of the entry without the event subprocesses and multi-instance
37 * activities within the performer, has not been dispatched, so that the order of the entries of every performer is
38 * kept. Every other decision is dispatched as soon as its request is pending. A decision is evaluated by the
39 * evaluator given before it is dispatched, which records its reward, and waits while it is infeasible. A decision dispatched later
40 * than its time is reported as a deviation. A decision whose request never becomes pending is not dispatched, so
41 * that the run continues until it ends otherwise and the playback is incomplete.
42 *
43 * The playback stops at the first mismatch, by a termination event: a choice lacking the value of a choice
44 * attribute, or an entry of an activity whose entry is not decided.
45 */
