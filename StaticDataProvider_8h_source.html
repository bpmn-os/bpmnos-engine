25/**
26 * @brief Data provider for instance data known from the start of a run.
27 *
28 * The data is read from a CSV table with the columns `INSTANCE_ID`, `NODE_ID` and `INITIALIZATION`. Every
29 * instance is instantiated at the first instant of a run, and its process becomes
30 * ready at its instantiation time, which is the timestamp of the instance rounded up. A token arriving at an
31 * activity becomes ready at once, with the status it arrived with followed by the values of the activity, and
32 * a task other than a send, receive or decision task completes with the status it became busy with at the
33 * timestamp of that status. An event due later is scheduled and enqueued when the @ref ClockTickEvent
34 * advancing to its time is announced. A run ends when nothing is left to do, no instance is left and no event
35 * is scheduled, or when nothing is left to do at the end time.
36 *
37 * The time at which every kind of event becomes due is given by a virtual method, which a derived data
38 * provider may override.
39 */
