BPMNOS::Execution::EntryObserver Class Reference

Enters the tokens at activities whose entry is not decided as soon as their entry restrictions hold. More...

Detailed Description

Only the entry of an activity within a sequential ad-hoc subprocess is decided, by the performer of the subprocess. Any other activity is entered as soon as the entry restrictions, those of scope entry or full and those inherited from the enclosing scopes, hold on the status the token has with the current time as its timestamp, as a conditional event is triggered as soon as its condition holds. A token whose restrictions do not hold waits among the tokens awaiting entry of the system state. It is checked again whenever a data update concerns an attribute its restrictions depend on and, if they depend on the timestamp, whenever time advances. A token whose restrictions never hold waits until the run ends.

The observer supplies one entry event at a time, which the engine dispatches before the events of the controller, so that the entry is notified and processed like any other event.
