41/**
42 * @brief A scenario implementation supporting stochastic behavior.
43 *
44 * StochasticScenario supports:
45 * - Random functions in INITIALIZATION, DISCLOSURE, and COMPLETION expressions
46 * - Per (instance, node) RNG for reproducibility
47 * - Downward compatible with static (3-column) and dynamic (4-column) CSV formats
48 * - Scenario copying with resampling of future values
49 *
50 * @par Resampling Behavior
51 * When a scenario is copied at a given spawnTime, values with disclosure time >= spawnTime
52 * are re-evaluated using a new RNG seed. If re-evaluated timestamps or disclosure times
53 * fall before spawnTime, they are resampled up to @ref maxResamplingTries times. If still
54 * in the past after all attempts, they fall back to spawnTime.
55 *
56 * @warning Resampling with fallback may alter the effective probability distribution,
57 * creating a point mass at spawnTime.
58 *
59 * @par Assumptions
60 * - CSV rows must be ordered with parent nodes before child nodes for dependent expressions
61 */
62class StochasticDataProvider;
