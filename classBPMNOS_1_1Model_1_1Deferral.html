BPMNOS::Model::Deferral Class Reference

The deferral of a decision point, permitting a controller to defer the decisions of the decision point. More...

Detailed Description

A deferral is stated in the message guidance of a catch event, in the choice guidance of a decision task, or in the extension elements of a performer element for the entries of the sequential activities of the performer. Its condition is a boolean expression deciding whether deferring is allowed, evaluated on the token of the decision point. Its observed attributes, each stated by a parameter named observable whose value is the name of a data or global attribute, time included, are the attributes whose update ends a deferral.
